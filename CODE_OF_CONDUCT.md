# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a respectful, constructive community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or the tools they use.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming language, discuss evidence and methodology in good faith, accept constructive feedback, and respect privacy and intellectual-property boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and knowingly submitting deceptive or unsafe benchmark material are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, reject, or moderate contributions that violate this code. For sensitive concerns, use GitHub's private reporting features when available rather than publishing personal information in an issue.
